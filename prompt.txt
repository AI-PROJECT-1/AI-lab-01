13. ENTAILMENT
============================================================

Đối với unresolved character i:

Nếu:

KB_t AND NOT Ci

UNSAT

=> CRIMINAL.

Nếu:

KB_t AND Ci

UNSAT

=> INNOCENT.

Nếu cả hai assumption SAT:

=> UNKNOWN.

Nếu KB_t bản thân UNSAT:

=> INCONSISTENT.

Một satisfying model KHÔNG đủ để kết luận verdict.

LogicAgent không được suy luận verdict chỉ dựa trên một SAT assignment.

33. FINAL PRIORITY
============================================================

Priority order:

1. Specification compliance
2. Logical correctness
3. Academic integrity
4. Testability
5. Explainability
6. Maintainability
7. Performance
8. Visual polish

A program that looks good but violates no-guess deduction is incorrect.

A solver that produces the correct final board by reading hidden
solution is incorrect.

A CNF encoder that passes one puzzle because of hard coding is
incorrect.

A report containing fabricated experiment values is incorrect.

Never trade correctness or integrity for apparent completeness.
